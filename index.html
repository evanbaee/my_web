About

I take ideas from a plan to a proven system — whatever the medium.

I'm Evan, a software engineer in Seoul and a Big Data student at Hallym University. I've been building software for nine years — freelancing from 2019 to 2025, and leading development at Camorix since 2025.

My work starts before the code. I plan what should exist, design the system behind it, then build and deploy it — on the web, as native software, or in whatever form the problem calls for. At Camorix that has meant Premind, an AI lecture platform, and Hailens, a classroom platform piloted at Hallym University.
